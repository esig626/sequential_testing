## Substantive changes from the earlier standalone plan

The existing order lambda and direction D_lambda(Q||P) replace the earlier standalone notation. The estimator is no longer denoted by an unqualified A, which would collide with the selected action. The horizon is T and all histories include decisions.

The target is the existing controlled joint path law and its G_t recursion. Generic product mixtures are no longer the starting point. Exact controlled models and integration over informative action branches come first; controlled mixtures and hidden observation models follow.

The common policy cancellation is derived without deleting its influence on future observations. The rare history example is rebuilt as a rare selected action. Current observation selection, full prefix accumulation, action projection and future continuation are separated explicitly.

Positive reach and exact zero support follow the existing relation note. Same data reuse is treated only as a boundary case. The two existing projects remain distinct.

The uncertainty discussion is strengthened: even with rectangular conditional kernels, a truth map fixed at each node cannot be optimised independently at every history reaching that node. The note contains a counterexample rather than silently changing the manuscript's truth definition.

Composite separation and path risk are not relabelled as consequences of a fixed pair estimator. Suggested future additions to the main formulation, including precise rectangular and coupled environment classes, remain proposals in the research documents.
